# DoD Cyber Sentinel Challenge

This folder contains artifacts and notes from participation in the **Department of Defense Cyber Sentinel Challenge**, a national-level cybersecurity competition held earlier this year. The event focused on real-world defensive and analytical scenarios across multiple cyber domains.

---

## Overview

**Objective:** Apply technical and analytical skills to detect, analyze, and respond to simulated cyber threats targeting DoD-style environments.  
**Format:** Multi-stage CTF competition featuring individual and team-based tasks in network defense, malware analysis, and threat intelligence.  
**Focus Areas:** Incident response, intrusion detection, reverse engineering, and secure communications.

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Threat hunting and log correlation  
- Malware triage and static analysis  
- Network traffic inspection and IOC extraction  
- Collaboration under operational time constraints  
- Reporting and documentation aligned with DoD standards  

---

> The Cyber Sentinel Challenge provided hands-on experience in mission-driven cybersecurity operations, emphasizing teamwork, precision, and real-world defense readiness.
