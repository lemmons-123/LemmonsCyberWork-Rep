### Why CIS 18?
- Previously ran by SANS (Now ran by CIS) and was called CIS 20; Evolved into CIS 18

This framework is good for small businesses relatively new or starting a security posture
- Not as comprehensive/flexible as NIST CSF which may be used for more established companies

Each control has a set # of safeguards:
IG1 Implementation: 1st tier of Implementation groups (least amount of safeguards implemented)
IG2 Imp. : 2nd tier (mid amount)
IG3 Imp. : Highest tier (All safeguards implemented)

### Audit Prep
1. Define Scope and Boundary
	- Systems, locations, etc.
2. Information Gathering
	- Network Diagrams
	- Policies and Procedures
	- Org/Char Key Stakeholders
		- Get the right people in the room when auditing
	- Prior Audit reports
3. Determine Team & Tools
	- Audit team
		- Who's help might you need?
	- Control Collection Doc
	- Audit Scripts - Taking the framework and make actionable steps to efficiently ask audit interview questions
		- Dr. As Strat: Take each Safeguard into a spreadsheet and have questions that go through each safeguard description
	- Evidence Management
		- Gotta take good notes!
