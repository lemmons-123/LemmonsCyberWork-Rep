# CprE 234 – Ethics in Cybersecurity

This folder contains recovered coursework from *CprE 234: Ethics in Cybersecurity* at Iowa State University. The course explored the ethical, legal, and societal implications of cybersecurity practices and technology use.

## Recovered Work: Threat Modeling in Industrial Control Systems

**Document:** Assignment 8 – Threat Modeling Analysis  
**Topic:** Applying ethical and technical reasoning to assess threats in critical infrastructure environments.  
**Focus Organization:** Saudi Aramco Oil Company  

### Overview
The paper examines how threat modeling frameworks such as **STRIDE**, **MITRE ATT&CK**, and **CAPEC** can be applied to industrial control systems (ICS) within the oil and gas sector. It discusses ethical responsibility in protecting human life, operational safety, and national infrastructure from cyber threats.

### Key Concepts Discussed
- Ethical considerations in securing high-risk environments  
- Threat actor analysis and potential impacts on safety and availability  
- Application of STRIDE for identifying spoofing, tampering, and denial-of-service risks  
- Integration of ATT&CK and CAPEC for classifying tactics and attack patterns  
- Importance of least privilege, IDS, and SIEM systems in mitigating threats  

### Skills and Perspectives Demonstrated
- Ethical decision-making in cybersecurity operations  
- Risk assessment and threat modeling methodology  
- Understanding of ICS vulnerabilities and real-world implications  
- Technical writing and structured analysis
