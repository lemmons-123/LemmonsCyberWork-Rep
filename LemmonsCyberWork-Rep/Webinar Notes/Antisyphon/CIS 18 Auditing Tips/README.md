# CIS Controls 18 Auditing – Prep to Reporting

This notesheet captures my summary from the Anti-Siphon “CIS Controls 18: Audit Prep to Reporting” webinar. It outlines how to prepare, structure, and execute a CIS 18 audit, emphasizing practical steps for evidence collection and stakeholder engagement.

## Why CIS 18
- Originally managed by SANS as “CIS 20,” now maintained by CIS and streamlined into 18 controls.  
- Ideal for small or growing organizations building a foundational security posture.  
- Less flexible than NIST CSF but easier to operationalize for newer programs.  
- Each control includes safeguards grouped by implementation tiers:  
  - **IG1:** Basic safeguards (entry-level)  
  - **IG2:** Intermediate safeguards  
  - **IG3:** Full implementation (comprehensive coverage)  

## Audit Preparation
1. **Define Scope and Boundary**  
   Identify systems, locations, and environments in scope.  

2. **Information Gathering**  
   - Collect network diagrams, policies, and procedures.  
   - Identify key stakeholders and ensure they’re involved in audit sessions.  
   - Review prior audit reports for context and recurring gaps.  

3. **Determine Team and Tools**  
   - Define the audit team and supporting roles.  
   - Use a **Control Collection Document** to track evidence.  
   - Build **Audit Scripts** that convert each safeguard into actionable interview questions.  
   - Maintain structured **Evidence Management** with detailed notes and references.
