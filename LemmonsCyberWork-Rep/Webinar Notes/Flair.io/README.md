# Flair.io Notes Collection

This repository contains research notes and summaries from the **Flair.io Cyber Threat Intelligence series**. Each entry focuses on major ransomware groups, negotiation tactics, and evolving threat trends. Notes are updated as new intelligence emerges.

## Completed Write-Ups

- **Conti & LockBit: The Big Players**
  - Comparative analysis of Conti’s corporate-style operations and LockBit’s cartel ambitions.
  - Covers affiliate structures, negotiation psychology, and branding strategies.
  - Includes insights from leaked chat logs and affiliate manuals.

## Key Topics Covered
1. **Conti** – Business-like ransomware model, insider leaks, and operational scale.
2. **LockBit** – Cartel structure, technical edge, and marketing-driven recruitment.
3. **Bassterlord** – LockBit affiliate who published detailed technical manuals.
4. **Ransom Negotiations** – Behavioral differences between Conti and LockBit.
5. **Modern Threat Landscape** – Rising families like QILIN, Madusa, and RansomLook.
6. **RansomChat Project** – AI simulation tool for practicing negotiation response.

## Goal
To build a concise, research-driven reference for understanding ransomware ecosystems, negotiation dynamics, and attacker psychology—useful for CTI analysts, IR teams, and security researchers.
